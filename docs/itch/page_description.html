"The sea remembers every journey."

Sail an ancient stitched-plank trading ship out of Muziris on the Malabar Coast of Kerala, read the monsoon, survive a squall, fight off the Hidden Cartographers, and use your missing father's bronze compass to SHIFT between the present and the sea's memory of the past.

A GitHub Game Off 2026 entry · Theme: SHIFT · Plays free in your browser · 15–20 minutes

Seven years ago the navigator Raghavan sailed west aboard the Vellimeen, searching for a route no chart recorded. He never returned.

His son Arivan inherited three things: a battered ship named the Kaveri, his father's bronze compass, and a promise to find the truth. Now Devaki of the Malabar Merchant Guild asks him to carry a sealed package across the western sea to the forgotten outpost of Ras al-Mira, before the monsoon shifts.

Fill your hold with pepper and cardamom, cross the open sea, and discover what the package really holds.

  • Wind Shift: the monsoon turns mid-voyage. A square sail needs the wind behind or beside it.
  • Weather Shift: a monsoon squall brings rain, lightning and towering waves.
  • Course Shift: abandon your heading and run for shelter behind Kadal Island.
  • Perspective Shift: stand at the helm and see the deck and sail up close.
  • Trade Shift: the storm closed the northern routes, so spices are worth three times more in the west.
  • Era Shift: your father's compass reveals the sea as it once was: ghost harbours, glowing echo stones and a hidden channel through the reefs.

New to games? Choose TUTORIAL VOYAGE on the title screen. A lesson panel teaches every button as you play, and a help line always tells you the best move. The tutorial also turns on assists: lighter damage, a stronger monsoon and gentle bumps instead of crashes.

Tips

  • Click the game once to take the helm. Your mouse then moves the camera.
  • The gold diamond always shows where to go. Press T and your crew will steer there for you.
  • You never land on islands. Every goal is reached by stopping near it on the water. Watch for the red SHALLOW WATER warning.
  • Your archers fire sideways: put the enemy beside your ship, then click.
  • Your hull repairs itself after 8 seconds without being hit.

  • A voyage across the ancient Indian Ocean, from Kerala's Malabar Coast to the Arabian shore
  • Arcade-sim sailing: square-sail physics, steering oar, monsoon winds and wave buoyancy
  • A small dynamic economy: buying raises prices, selling lowers them
  • Fire-arrow broadsides, ramming, burning hulls and sinking ships
  • Dynamic weather and time of day: golden dawn, midday sun, storm, sunset and dusk
  • A fully guided Tutorial Voyage for first-time players
  • Original music inspired by Kerala traditions: tanpura drone, a bansuri-style flute in Raga Mohanam, chenda and mizhavu drums, and a conch horn

Built in Unity 6 for the web. No imported assets: every ship, palm tree, Kerala house, temple, ruin and island, every texture, sound effect and note of music is generated by code when the game loads. Custom shaders draw the Gerstner-wave ocean with shore foam and ship wakes, the sky with clouds, stars and lightning, the billowing sails and the golden Era Shift memory effect.
